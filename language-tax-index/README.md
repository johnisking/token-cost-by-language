# TokenSave Language Tax Index

Extra tokens the same text needs in 40 languages versus English, per tokenizer. Version 1.2 (2026-10-21): median +41% on Gemini 3.x and Gemini 4 Argon, +52% on GPT (o200k), +88% on Claude 5.5 (Opus, Sonnet, Haiku), +108% on Claude Haiku 4.5, +129% on the legacy GPT-4 tokenizer (cl100k).

New in v1.2: Gemini 4 Argon has a new tokenizer. Same median as Gemini 3.x and English unchanged at 34 tokens, but Korean fell 15% (48 to 41 tokens) and Japanese 9%, while Kannada rose 24%, Hindi 17% and Tamil 21%.
